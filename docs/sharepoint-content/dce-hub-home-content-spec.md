## Page purpose

The DCE Hub Home page should be the staff-facing starting point for Delta Crown resources, operating materials, brand updates, and service paths.

Recommended positioning:

> Delta Crown's internal home base for brand, operations, training, and corporate service resources.

## Audience posture

Default audience: DCE staff and approved HTT/DCE support users.

Owner-only or franchisor-only content should route to Crown Connection or explicitly audience-targeted sections. Do not assume all hub visitors are franchise owners.

## People / owner spotlight

The mockup includes an owner spotlight. Treat this as optional and approval-gated.

Do not publish:

- owner names;
- booking metrics;
- performance claims;
- personal bios;
- store-specific shoutouts;

unless the featured person and business owner approve the copy.

Recommended safer placeholder:

> Spotlight content will be added after brand approval.

## Events

The mockup includes upcoming events. Treat event cards as placeholder until the calendar source is confirmed.

Candidate event types:

- owner roundtables;
- training cohorts;
- launch/readiness calls;
- brand update sessions.

Need source of truth:

- Outlook group calendar;
- SharePoint events list;
- Teams channel calendar;
- manual page section.

## KPI tiles

The mockup includes operational KPI tiles. Do not include production KPI tiles until the source, owner, and audience are approved.

Risks:

- stale or manually-entered metrics;
- visibility to wrong audience;
- confusion between demo and actual operating data.

Recommendation:

> Defer KPI tiles until the reporting source and intended audience are approved.
