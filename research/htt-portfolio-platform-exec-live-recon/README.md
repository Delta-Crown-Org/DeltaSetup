# HTT Portfolio Platform Executive Live Recon

Focused Tier 3 live current-state research for an executive intro Reveal.js deck for HTT Brands / Delta Crown Extensions.

## Executive summary

Live recon confirms the canonical deck direction should remain intact: HTT as relationship-first parent platform; DCE/Crown as modern repeatable luxury; Frenchies as clean warm care; The Lash Lounge as confidence/luxe customization; Bishops as bold approachable individuality.

No observed live-source issue warrants replacing canonical logos or brand-book tokens. The main deck adjustment is usage discipline: keep the executive deck dark and contrast-safe, use accent colors as accents/fills rather than small text, and bring in live current-state photography and voice to make the deck feel current.

## Key findings

- **HTT Brands:** maroon/white parent identity, warm hero photography, relationship-first multi-brand growth language.
- **DCE/Crown:** deltacrown.com redirects to crownextensionstudio.com. Live site emphasizes gold/teal, premium salon service, consultation, membership/Crown Society, and warm service photography.
- **Frenchies:** canonical warm cream/navy/coral direction is validated; live headline “Not just a nail salon, a revolution.” is highly deck-ready.
- **The Lash Lounge:** live site uses plum/amethyst/mauve, split hero, circular lash close-up, and confidence-led copy. This is a useful refinement to the black-canvas canonical context.
- **Bishops:** canonical orange/black/white is strongly validated; current hero uses bold all-caps, halftone/collage photography, and approachable rebellious copy.

## Deck-ready recommendation

Use the deck to show **shared platform leverage with preserved brand magnetism**. Do not homogenize. Introduce HTT as the system, then show each brand’s live energy through a disciplined, accessible, dark executive palette.

## Accessibility caveats

- Several live sites use accessibility overlay widgets; do not treat those as WCAG proof.
- DCE gold and many warm accent colors should not be used as small text on light backgrounds.
- Place text on solid panels or scrims over photography.
- Use reduced-motion support in Reveal.js and avoid autoplay motion.
