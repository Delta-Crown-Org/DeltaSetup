# Deck-Ready Recommendations

## Bottom line

No live recon revealed a serious accessibility issue that requires replacing canonical logos or brand-book tokens. Keep canonical identity assets. Use live sites only to tune slide mood, photography, copy, and micro-interactions.

## Recommended executive deck treatment

1. **Parent frame: HTT as relationship-first growth platform**
   - Title direction: “Multiple Brands. One Powerful Support System.”
   - Visual: dark executive canvas with HTT maroon/white and subtle warm gold accents; use a warm salon/business hero crop with a maroon overlay.
   - CTA/narrative line: “Build the platform without flattening the brands.”

2. **DCE/Crown: keep canonical dark luxury, add live consumer warmth**
   - Keep repo/canonical DCE teal + gold luxury system and current logos.
   - Add live-informed content: “Premium Hair Extension Salon,” “Crown Society,” “The New Guest Experience,” and consultation/member language.
   - Photography: use stylist/guest salon consultation or service-space imagery; avoid pure product shots. Crop warm, human, operationally repeatable.
   - CTA copy options: “Set the Crown Standard,” “Reserve the Consultation,” “Scale the Crown Society.”

3. **Brand cards should express difference, not uniformity**
   - Frenchies: cream/navy/coral, warm/editorial nail care, “Not just a nail salon, a revolution.”
   - TLL: plum/black/white, circular/detail lash photography, “Choose Lashes, Invest in You.”
   - Bishops: black/white/orange, bold all-caps, halftone/collage, “Come as you are.”
   - HTT: maroon/white, relationship-first franchise economics.

4. **Motion system for Reveal.js**
   - Use restrained executive motion: fade-up for proof points; staggered brand cards; one image mask reveal for DCE/Crown.
   - Do not recreate live-site autoplay sliders. Use manual slide progression only.
   - Include reduced-motion CSS (`prefers-reduced-motion`) and avoid essential information conveyed by animation alone.

5. **WCAG AA substitutions / guardrails**
   - Use near-white text on dark teal/near-black for body copy.
   - Use DCE gold, HTT yellow, Frenchies peach/coral, and Bishops orange primarily as fills, dividers, badges, and large CTA elements—not small light-background text.
   - If a brand accent must become text, pair it with a dark enough background and test contrast.
   - Add solid or gradient scrims behind text over photography; do not place white or gold type directly over bright salon images.
   - Preserve visible focus styles for deck controls/links.
