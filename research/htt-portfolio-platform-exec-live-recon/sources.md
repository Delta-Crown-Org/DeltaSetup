## Validation and bias notes

- These are official commercial sites; they are authoritative for what the market sees today but commercially biased toward consumer acquisition/franchise conversion.
- They should be treated as current-state recon, not as replacements for internal brand books or canonical deck context.
- Multiple sources cross-validate the portfolio pattern: all brands use high-contrast hero photography, short confident CTAs, and acquisition-oriented top-level copy.
- Accessibility caveat: several sites use accessiBe overlays. Overlay presence does not guarantee WCAG conformance and should not be used as evidence that deck contrast/keyboard/motion requirements are satisfied.
