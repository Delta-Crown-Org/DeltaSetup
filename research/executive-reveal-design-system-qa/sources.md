## Notes on bias and validation

- Accessibility recommendations are grounded in W3C/WAI primary sources and cross-checked with local token contrast calculations.
- Reveal.js implementation recommendations are grounded in official framework docs.
- Brand observations are not a substitute for legal/brand-book rules; they validate public-facing tone, color direction, and logo/background usage.
